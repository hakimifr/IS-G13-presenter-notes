## Time flex

Short version (about 32 minutes, cut about 14 minutes):
- Presenter 1: skip slides 8 and 10 (glossary and forms). Say the key terms in passing.
- Presenter 2: skip slide 19 (world models) and run only the first case question on slide 21.
- Presenter 3: show only one verse on each of slides 26 and 27, skip slide 28, and combine slides 30 and 31 in one minute.
- Presenter 4: skip slide 41 (second myth slide) and skip the quiz (slides 43 and 44).
- Presenter 5: use only one case card on slide 53 and skip slide 49.

Long version (about 50 minutes, add about 4 minutes):
- Let the audience answer the poll on slide 6 and the case on slide 21 in full.
- Presenter 5 runs all three case cards on slide 53.
- Presenter 4 asks one volunteer to explain each quiz answer.

## Language and tone rules for the whole group

- This is a sensitive topic. Use "people in prostitution" or "the woman" or "the victim" and avoid insulting words for individuals.
- We condemn the act and the exploitation firmly. We do not condemn or mock the person. That is the theme of the whole talk, from the story on slide 4 to the conclusion.
- Do not read verses from the slide in a rush. Recite the Arabic slowly if you can, then give the meaning.
- Say "peace be upon him" (the salawat) after the Prophet's name, as you normally would. The slides write it as (SAW).
- Every number in the deck has its source on the slide footer and on the reference slides. If someone challenges a number, say where it comes from and that it is an estimate.
