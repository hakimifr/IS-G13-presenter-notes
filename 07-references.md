## Notes on the evidence

- Numbers about prostitution come from a hidden population, so they are estimates and the studies have limits. We say this in the talk.
- The Farley study is widely cited, but its lead author is a known critic of prostitution and the sample came mostly through agencies and the street.
- The Cho, Dreher and Neumayer study compares countries and shows correlation, not proof of cause for each country.
- The HIV figures come from Greater Kuala Lumpur samples collected in 2014 and 2015, and may not match today's rates.
