## Language and tone rules for the whole group

- This is a sensitive topic. Use "people in prostitution" or "the woman" or "the victim" and avoid insulting words for individuals.
- We condemn the act and the exploitation firmly. We do not condemn or mock the person. That is the theme of the whole talk, from the story on slide 4 to the conclusion.
- Do not read verses from the slide in a rush. Recite the Arabic slowly if you can, then give the meaning.
- Say "peace be upon him" (the salawat) after the Prophet's name, as you normally would. The slides write it as (SAW).
- Verse references are written "Surah An-Nur: Verse 33" on the slides. Say it as "Surah An-Nur, verse 33".
- Every number in the deck has its source on the slide footer and on the reference slides. If someone challenges a number, say where it comes from and that it is an estimate.
